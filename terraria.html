🌲 SURVIVAL & COMBAT GUIDE

OBJECTIVES

  • Daytime: Gather Wood, Stone, Iron & Gold ore. Craft stronger weapons (Broadsword, Starlight Bow, Forest Wand), armor and torches.
  • Nighttime: At 07:00 PM the sky turns dark. Zombies, Demon Eyes, and Forest Wraiths spawn!
  • The Underworld: Dig far below the blackstone layer. You will reach a lava ocean and the Hellbound Castle. Its gate opens with one click; the demon altar inside wakes a three-phase boss.
  • The Boss: Surviving to midnight or clicking [👁️ SUMMON BOSS] awakens the Ancient Forest Guardian! Defeat both phases (Enraged Eye & Bramble Behemoth) to save the forest.

THINGS TO DO

  • 🎣 Go fishing. Craft a Fishing Rod (8 wood + 2 wool), select it, and right-click a pool of water to cast. Wait for the bobber to flash, then press A or D to reel it in. Fish feed you; Golden Koi and Sunken Boots sell the dream. Every catch raises your Angler level, which attracts rarer fish.
  • 📖 Fill your Journal. Press J to see your settlement score — days survived, monsters slain, bosses defeated, chests opened, quests finished. The first time you do something, the game announces it and logs it forever.
  • 📦 Use the Shared Stash. Open your bag with I. Right-click an item (or Ctrl+click, or hold Shift for the whole stack) to store it. Those items follow you into every world you make.
  • 🧭 Talk to villagers (T) for quests, explore the swamp for the drowned chapel, and wake the Cursed Knight inside it for a second boss.
  • 💗 Grow stronger: craft Life & Mana Crystals to permanently raise your caps, then chase Rainbow Ore for the Prismatic Saber and Aurora Blade.

CONTROLS & MOVEMENT

  • A / D — Smooth run & slide mechanics.
  • W or SPACE — Jump. Press again in air for double jump (Hermes Wind).
  • S — Crouch / Fast fall through wood platforms.
  • SHIFT — Dodge roll with invincibility frames & particle trail!
  • Wall Slide & Wall Jump — Touch any wall while falling and press SPACE to wall kick!
  • Left Click — Swing sword, shoot bow, cast spells, or mine tiles with pickaxe.
  • Right Click — Place blocks or consume potions.
  • 1 - 9 or Mouse Scroll — Switch hotbar slot.
  • E — Open/Close Crafting Forge.
  • T — Talk to nearby villagers (Guide, Prospector, Scavenger) and accept quests.
  • C — Open the Creative Menu and give yourself any item or block.
  • Q — Drink the best buff potion in your bag.
  • H — Quick heal with a potion.
  • M — Cycle the minimap: hidden → small → medium → large.
  • O — Open Settings for quality, effects density, audio, saves, and quick actions.
  • F — Toggle the performance readout (FPS, resolution, effect count).
  • J — Open the Settlement Journal (discoveries and score).
  • Right-click with the Fishing Rod on water — cast or reel in a line.

PERFORMANCE

  • Terracraft watches its own frame time. If your machine starts to struggle it quietly drops to half resolution and halves the number of effects — the game stays smooth and simply looks a little chunkier.
  • It returns to full resolution on its own once frames are fast again. Press F to see what it is doing.
  • The F readout shows FPS, the average frame time this machine actually spends, the current resolution, and how many effects are live. FPS is the real display rate; frame time is what tells you whether the work itself is heavy.
