🌲 SURVIVAL & COMBAT GUIDE

THE OCEAN REEF (the huge western sea)

  • The ocean reef is the whole LEFT (west) edge of the world — a deep blue band east of nothing and west of the Frostpine snow. Pirates sometimes spawn in it (more often at night) and drop Coral Fragments. Fish it for Clownfish, Parrotfish, Lionfish, Turtle, Manta and Anglerfish.
  • Dive first. Without gear you get 8 seconds of air underwater. Craft three tiers of diving gear — Reef Diver Set (18s), Tidecaller Set (38s), Abyssal Suit (75s) — and three tiers of swim fins (Reef → Current → Leviathan) for speed. The early tiers cost overworld wool/iron/fish; the top tiers want Coral, Manta, Anglerfish and Sacred Pearls. Your air is safe inside the Tide Temple. The OXYGEN bar appears whenever your head is under water outside its sanctuary.
  • Find the drowned Tide Temple. Its ancient stone arch, carved towers and glowing gate stand deep in the reef. Four Sacred Pearls are scattered across the world; do favors for the Tidekeeper, Frost Scout, Sun Seeker and Marsh Warden to get their exact coordinates. Bring the pearls to the temple and right-click one pearl onto each of the four podiums — two on either side of the portal. When all four offerings are placed, the gate lights up and opens the way to the ocean planet.
  • Beware the reef shark. Sharks patrol the open water and will chase you through the reef. Defeat one for Coral Fragments, with a chance of a Manta.
  • The Tide Gate leads to an ocean planet — all water, no land — where the THREE-HEADED SEA LEVIATHAN (110,000 HP) rises the moment you arrive. Its water lasers and tidal bursts hit hardest from the middle head, which also shrugs off more damage than the flanks: break the side heads first, then the crown. The more it loses, the more wounded it looks.
  • Ask the Old Sailor at the spawn camp — he knows the temple ritual and the Tidekeeper can be found on the reef shore. The Star Watcher next to him tells you how to tear the sky open with a Void Rift Beacon and what waits in the Ossuary beyond.

WAKING THE SOVEREIGN (the Ossuary ritual)

  • The final boss does NOT spawn on its own. The Skeleton Dragon is sealed in the Ossuary until you perform the rite yourself. Arriving in the arena finds a quiet, dark, mineable grave — that first visit is for getting ready, not for fighting.
  • 1. Craft a Void Rift Beacon (4 Demon Souls, 6 Hellstone, 4 Crystal, 8 Gold Ore) and right-click it under open sky. The beacon is a door, never a summons — it will not wake anything.
  • 2. Scout the Ossuary. Mine the bone piles (Dragonbone), the 🟠 meteor seams and the magenta nebula clusters. The Ossuary Deep below the arena floor is worth digging into: the deeper you go, the richer the veins.
  • 3. Come home and craft the Rite of Waking (10 Dragonbone, 8 Meteor Shards, 4 Nebula Crystals). Press E at a crafting forge.
  • 4. Go back in and right-click the candle in the arena. Right-click the 🕯️ Rite of Waking while standing in the Ossuary — the bones burn and the Sovereign rises. This is the only thing that starts the fight.
  • After you have killed one it stays dead, and the Ossuary becomes a pure quarry. To fight it again you need another Rite of Waking, and it costs far more the second time: 34 Dragonbone, 26 Meteor Shards, 18 Nebula Crystals, 20 Crystals, 6 Demon Souls and 2 Life Crystals.

OBJECTIVES

  • Daytime: Gather Wood, Stone, Iron & Gold ore. Craft stronger weapons (Broadsword, Starlight Bow, Forest Wand), armor and torches.
  • Nighttime: At 07:00 PM the sky turns dark. Zombies, Demon Eyes, and Forest Wraiths spawn!
  • 🍎 Eat when you need it. Use food from your hotbar to restore hunger and life; you cannot eat when both are full. Potions can be used back-to-back, and each potion occupies its own inventory slot.
  • The Underworld: Dig far below the blackstone layer. You will reach a lava ocean and the Hellbound Castle. Its gate opens with one click; the demon altar inside wakes a three-phase boss.
  • The Boss: Surviving to midnight or clicking [👁️ SUMMON BOSS] awakens the Ancient Forest Guardian! Defeat both phases (Enraged Eye & Bramble Behemoth) to save the forest.

THINGS TO DO

  • 🎣 Go fishing. Craft a Fishing Rod (8 wood + 2 wool), select it, and right-click a pool of water to cast. Wait for the bobber to flash, then press A or D to reel it in. Fish feed you; Golden Koi and Sunken Boots sell the dream. Every catch raises your Angler level, which attracts rarer fish.
  • 📖 Fill your Journal. Press J to see your settlement score — days survived, monsters slain, bosses defeated, chests opened, quests finished. The first time you do something, the game announces it and logs it forever.
  • 📦 Use the Shared Stash. Open your bag with I. Right-click an item (or Ctrl+click, or hold Shift for the whole stack) to store it. Those items follow you into every world you make.
  • 🧭 Talk to villagers (T) for quests, explore the swamp for the drowned chapel, and wake the Cursed Knight inside it for a second boss.
  • 💗 Grow stronger: craft Life & Mana Crystals to permanently raise your caps, then chase Rainbow Ore for the Prismatic Saber and Aurora Blade.

CONTROLS & MOVEMENT

  • A / D — Smooth run & slide mechanics.
  • SPACE — Jump. Press again in air for double jump (Hermes Wind).
  • S — Crouch / Fast fall through wood platforms.
  • SHIFT — Dodge roll with invincibility frames & particle trail!
  • CTRL — Sprint while moving.
  • Wall Slide & Wall Jump — Touch any wall while falling and press SPACE to wall kick!
  • Left Click — Swing sword, shoot bow, cast spells, or mine tiles with pickaxe.
  • Right Click — Place blocks or consume potions.
  • 1-9 or Mouse Scroll — Switch hotbar slot.
  • E — Open/Close Crafting Forge.
  • T — Talk to nearby villagers (Guide, Prospector, Scavenger) and accept quests.
  • C — Open the Creative Menu and give yourself any item or block.
  • Q — Drink the best buff potion in your bag.
  • G — Drop the held stack four blocks ahead. Shift+G drops just one item out of a stack.
  • H — Quick heal with a potion.
  • M — Cycle the minimap: hidden → small → medium → large.
  • O — Open Settings for quality, effects density, audio, saves, and quick actions.
  • ESC or P — Pause menu: Resume, Save World, Settings, or Main Menu. Main Menu saves your world and drops you back at the title screen so you can switch save files without closing the game.
  • F — Toggle the performance readout (FPS, resolution, effect count).
  • I — Open your inventory.
  • J — Open the Settlement Journal (discoveries and score).
  • Right-click with the Fishing Rod on water — cast or reel in a line.

PLAYING ON A PHONE OR TABLET

  • On your first visit the title screen asks how you are playing. Pick Mobile and on-screen thumb controls appear over the world; pick PC / Laptop and you get the keyboard and mouse bindings above. The answer is remembered — change it any time in Settings → Controls, or with the small 🎮 chip in the corner of the title screen.
  • ◀ ▶ — move. ⤒ — jump (tap again in the air to double jump). ⤓ — drop through a platform. 💨 — dodge roll.
  • ⛏️ — attack, mine or interact, held down to keep swinging. ✋ — place the block in your hand, drink a potion, cast the rod.
  • Tap the world to act on it. Whatever you are holding decides what a tap does: a block appears under your fingertip, a pickaxe mines, a sword swings, a potion gets drunk. The crosshair shows where the next tap lands — green means it will work, red means it is too far away or already occupied. You can place above, below and beside you, not just in one spot.
  • A fingertip is far less precise than a mouse, so when you are holding a weapon and tap near a monster the aim snaps onto it — combat gets forgiving. Mining stays exact.
  • ⏸ opens the pause menu (Resume, Save World, Settings, Main Menu). ❤ quick-heals, 🧪 drinks the best buff potion, 💬 talks to a nearby villager.
  • The HUD is drawn smaller in this mode so the controls have room to sit; the main menu is left at full size. You can also set the HUD size yourself under Settings → Display → HUD size (Small / Normal / Large) — that one is yours on any device and never touches the main menu either.

PERFORMANCE

  • Terracraft watches its own frame time. If your machine starts to struggle it quietly drops to half resolution and halves the number of effects — the game stays smooth and simply looks a little chunkier.
  • It returns to full resolution on its own once frames are fast again. Press F to see what it is doing.
  • The F readout shows FPS, the average frame time this machine actually spends, the current resolution, and how many effects are live. FPS is the real display rate; frame time is what tells you whether the work itself is heavy.

Multiplayer

Play in one shared world with up to 3 friends. The host keeps the world and save; friends join as guests.

  1. Pick how you are connecting below. For other houses, you need a multiplayer server address from the person hosting it.
  2. Everyone enters the same 4-character room code and their own name.
  3. One person clicks HOST GAME. Friends click JOIN GAME using that same code.
Simple connection guide (tap to read)

Playing on one computer: leave Server address blank. Open the game in two browser tabs. In the first tab, enter a room code and click HOST GAME. In the second tab, enter the same code and click JOIN GAME.

Playing on different computers: you need a multiplayer server address that is already set up and running. Ask the person who set it up for the full address, then every player enters that exact address, the same room code, and clicks HOST GAME or JOIN GAME as appropriate. The room code alone cannot connect computers.

Do not have a server address? You can still play using two tabs on one computer. The hosted web page does not automatically create an internet server, so players in different homes need the server address first.

The host starts in their current world. Friends appear in that world after joining. Press T (or Enter) to chat.
